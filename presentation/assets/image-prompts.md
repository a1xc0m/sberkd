# Иллюстрации презентации

Созданы встроенным ImageGen в режиме генерации новых растровых изображений (`photorealistic-natural`). Файлы сохранены в проекте.

## `family-cover.png`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 presentation title image. A parent and an 8-year-old child at a modern home kitchen table, warm natural daylight, discussing a small toy construction set while looking at a phone held by the parent. Group both people and toy on the right half and leave clear dark-neutral negative space on the left half for a slide title. Refined candid editorial photography, subtle deep green accents. No text, no logo, no visible app UI, no money, no additional people.

## `family-story.png`

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: wide 16:9 title-slide visual for a Russian family financial literacy product pitch. Primary request: candid warm scene of a parent and an 8-year-old child sitting together at a kitchen table, happily discussing a small toy construction set and looking at a phone; the adult is visibly engaged and in control of the phone, the child points to the toy. Scene/backdrop: modern comfortable home in daylight. Style/medium: refined editorial photography, natural and believable, no advertising gloss. Composition: people and toy grouped on the right half, substantial calm negative space on the left half for slide title. Color palette: warm neutrals with subtle deep green accents. Text: none. Constraints: no visible app interface, no logos, no brand marks, no money or banknotes, no additional people, age-appropriate child, natural anatomy and hands.
